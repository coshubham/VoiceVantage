Quick answer

ElevenLabs, widely regarded as one of the most realistic and inexpensive voice cloners across many languages, did not pass VoiceCheck at all. Its highest score was 45, below the 60 test threshold and far below the 79 to 89 range genuine users reach. It was blocked outright on laptop, cell phone, and landline audio.

Verdict

Blocked

Laptop (best 45), cell phone (best 31) and landline (best 9) clones stayed below the pass line of 60. No clone passed on any channel.

AI voice cloning tools like ElevenLabs can copy a voice from seconds of audio, so a fair question follows: can an ElevenLabs clone slip past VoiceVantage? To answer it with evidence, the Institute for Cyber Security and Resilient Systems at Ontario Tech University (UOIT) tested VoiceCheck against the five most popular deepfake tools, including ElevenLabs. This page shows ElevenLabs's results in full.

How the test worked

1. Clone the voice

ElevenLabs was used to create deepfake copies of enrolled users' voices.

2. Try to log in

The clones tried to pass as the real user over a laptop microphone, a cell phone and an 8kHz landline.

3. VoiceCheck decides

Scores below 60 are rejected. A low pass under the 79 to 89 genuine range goes to a Help Desk.

Inside the 533-test study

The Ontario Tech study ran in two phases across all five deepfake tools. In every case, a score of 60 that stays below the 79 to 89 genuine range is referred to a Help Desk, not accepted.

What these results mean

This is the clearest result of the five. ElevenLabs is widely regarded as one of the most realistic and inexpensive voice cloners, yet it did not pass VoiceCheck on any channel. Its highest score was 45, below the 60 test threshold and far below the 79 to 89 range genuine users reach.

On landline audio, the quality where the other tools occasionally reached the low 60s, ElevenLabs scored near zero. Against VoiceCheck, the strongest consumer cloner was blocked outright.

Where this protection matters

Contact centers

Callers are verified by voice in about 1.4 milliseconds, so agents are not left to judge a cloned voice by ear.

Banks and payments

A voice check before a payment or an account change adds a step that a cloned voice has to beat, and in testing it did not.

Help desks and resets

Password resets are a favourite target for voice fraud. Low scores go to a person for a second check instead of passing.
