Quick answer

FakeYou was tested against VoiceCheck and blocked within its recommended configuration. Its clones only crossed the 60 test threshold on low landline audio, scoring 66 at best, well below the 79 to 89 range genuine users reach and referred to a Help Desk in production. On laptop and cell phone audio FakeYou did not pass, and it failed to pronounce some names accurately.

Verdict

Blocked, with Help Desk check

Laptop (best 50) and cell phone (best 22) clones stayed below the pass line of 60. Low-quality landline (best 66) passes go to a Help Desk, not straight in.

AI voice cloning tools like FakeYou can copy a voice from seconds of audio, so a fair question follows: can a FakeYou clone slip past VoiceVantage? To answer it with evidence, the Institute for Cyber Security and Resilient Systems at Ontario Tech University (UOIT) tested VoiceCheck against the five most popular deepfake tools, including FakeYou. This page shows FakeYou's results in full.

How the test worked

1. Clone the voice

FakeYou was used to create deepfake copies of enrolled users' voices.

2. Try to log in

The clones tried to pass as the real user over a laptop microphone, a cell phone and an 8kHz landline.

3. VoiceCheck decides

Scores below 60 are rejected. A low pass under the 79 to 89 genuine range goes to a Help Desk.

Inside the 533-test study

The Ontario Tech study ran in two phases across all five deepfake tools. In every case, a score of 60 that stays below the 79 to 89 genuine range is referred to a Help Desk, not accepted.

What these results mean

The pattern is clear. FakeYou only approached the pass line at the lowest audio quality, a landline call, where its best clone scored 66. That sits far below the 79 to 89 band genuine users reach, and in a live deployment any score in that range is referred to a Help Desk. On laptop and cell phone audio, FakeYou clones did not pass.

FakeYou also struggled with accuracy. In testing it failed to pronounce some participant names correctly, which drove several attempts to zero. That makes it one of the weaker threats among the five tools, despite its large library of voices.

Where this protection matters

Contact centers

Callers are verified by voice in about 1.4 milliseconds, so agents are not left to judge a cloned voice by ear.

Banks and payments

A voice check before a payment or an account change adds a step that a cloned voice has to beat, and in testing it did not.

Help desks and resets

Password resets are a favourite target for voice fraud. Low scores go to a person for a second check instead of passing.

About FakeYou

FakeYou is a user-friendly platform that offers text-to-speech and voice-to-voice cloning along with a large library of voice packs. Clone quality depends on how much training audio is provided.

Frequently asked questions

Can FakeYou voice clones bypass VoiceVantage?

In independent UOIT testing, FakeYou was blocked within VoiceCheck's recommended configuration. Its clones only crossed the 60 test threshold on low landline audio, scoring 66 at best, below the 79 to 89 range genuine users reach and referred to a Help Desk in production.

What was FakeYou's highest score against VoiceVantage?

66, on a low-quality landline attempt. On laptop and cell phone audio FakeYou clones did not pass, and the tool failed to pronounce some names accurately.

Is VoiceVantage safe against AI voice cloning?

Yes, within its recommended configuration. Across 533 independent attempts using all five leading deepfake tools, the pass rate was very low, and passing scores were confined to low landline quality and referred to a Help Desk.

How was this tested?

By the Institute for Cyber Security and Resilient Systems at Ontario Tech University (UOIT), using FakeYou to generate deepfake voice clones and attempting to authenticate against VoiceCheck across laptop, cell phone, and landline channels.
