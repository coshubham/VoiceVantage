Quick answer

Resemble AI was blocked within VoiceCheck's recommended configuration. Its clones crossed the 60 test threshold only on low landline audio, scoring 66 at best, below the 79 to 89 range genuine users reach and referred to a Help Desk in production. On laptop and cell phone audio it did not pass.

Verdict

Blocked, with Help Desk check

Laptop (best 49) and cell phone (best 46) clones stayed below the pass line of 60. Low-quality landline (best 66) passes go to a Help Desk, not straight in.

AI voice cloning tools like Resemble AI can copy a voice from seconds of audio, so a fair question follows: can a Resemble AI clone slip past VoiceVantage? To answer it with evidence, the Institute for Cyber Security and Resilient Systems at Ontario Tech University (UOIT) tested VoiceCheck against the five most popular deepfake tools, including Resemble AI. This page shows Resemble AI's results in full.

How the test worked

1. Clone the voice

Resemble AI was used to create deepfake copies of enrolled users' voices.

2. Try to log in

The clones tried to pass as the real user over a laptop microphone, a cell phone and an 8kHz landline.

3. VoiceCheck decides

Scores below 60 are rejected. A low pass under the 79 to 89 genuine range goes to a Help Desk.

Inside the 533-test study

The Ontario Tech study ran in two phases across all five deepfake tools. In every case, a score of 60 that stays below the 79 to 89 genuine range is referred to a Help Desk, not accepted.

What these results mean

Resemble AI reached the pass line only on low landline audio, with a best score of 66, below the genuine 79 to 89 range and Help-Desk-referred in production. On laptop and cell phone audio it did not pass.

Resemble AI also adds inaudible audio watermarking to the clones it produces. Where a system can read those watermarks, misuse becomes traceable, an extra signal on top of VoiceCheck's own detection.

Where this protection matters

Contact centers

Callers are verified by voice in about 1.4 milliseconds, so agents are not left to judge a cloned voice by ear.

Banks and payments

A voice check before a payment or an account change adds a step that a cloned voice has to beat, and in testing it did not.

Help desks and resets

Password resets are a favourite target for voice fraud. Low scores go to a person for a second check instead of passing.

About Resemble AI

Resemble AI offers instant and higher-fidelity voice clones and adds inaudible audio watermarking to its output so that clones can be traced where the watermark is supported.

Frequently asked questions

Can Resemble AI voice clones bypass VoiceVantage?

In independent UOIT testing, Resemble AI was blocked within VoiceCheck's recommended configuration. Its clones only reached the 60 test threshold on low landline audio, scoring 66 at best, below the genuine range and referred to a Help Desk.

Does Resemble AI watermarking help?

Resemble AI adds inaudible watermarks to its clones, so misuse can be traced where a system reads them. That is an extra signal on top of VoiceCheck's own detection.

Is VoiceVantage safe against AI voice cloning?

Yes, within its recommended configuration. Across 533 independent attempts using all five leading deepfake tools, the pass rate was very low, and passing scores were confined to low landline quality and referred to a Help Desk.

How was this tested?
