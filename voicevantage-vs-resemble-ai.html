AI voice cloning tools like Resemble AI can copy a person's voice from seconds of audio, so a fair question follows: can a cloned voice simply walk past VoiceVantage? To answer it with evidence rather than claims, the Institute for Cyber Security and Resilient Systems at Ontario Tech University (UOIT) tested VoiceCheck against the five most popular deepfake tools, including Resemble AI. This page shows Resemble AI's results in full.

How the test worked

1. Clone the voice

Resemble AI was used to create deepfake copies of enrolled users' voices.

2. Try to log in

The clones tried to pass as the real user over a laptop microphone, a cell phone and an 8kHz landline.

3. VoiceCheck decides

Scores below 60 are rejected. A low pass under the 79 to 89 genuine range goes to a Help Desk.

Inside the 533-test study

The Ontario Tech study ran in two phases across all five deepfake tools. In every case, a score of 60 that stays below the 79 to 89 genuine range is referred to a Help Desk, not accepted.

Where this protection matters

Contact centers

Callers are verified by voice in about 1.4 milliseconds, so agents are not left to judge a cloned voice by ear.

Banks and payments

A voice check before a payment or an account change adds a step that a cloned voice has to beat, and in testing it did not.

Help desks and resets

Password resets are a favourite target for voice fraud. Low scores go to a person for a second check instead of passing.

About Resemble AI

Resemble AI is a voice cloning platform built for developers and businesses. It can create a custom voice from recorded speech and generate new speech through an API, which is exactly the kind of automated, scalable cloning that makes voice fraud a real concern for call centers.

Why VoiceVantage blocks Resemble AI deepfakes

VoiceVantage validates a voice in as little as 1.4 milliseconds and is built on many years of signal-processing research.
